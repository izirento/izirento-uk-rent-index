## Why CC BY 4.0 fits the source licences

| Source | Its licence | Condition | How this dataset meets it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ONS (rents) | Open Government Licence v3.0 | Acknowledge the source; link to the licence; use the provider's statement if one is given; do not suggest endorsement; do not mislead about the information | Attribution below, URL columns in the CSVs, README states values are rounded |
| MHCLG, GOV.UK pages (England council tax, landlord guidance) | OGL v3.0 | Same as above | Same |
| Welsh Government (Wales council tax) | OGL | Same as above | Same |
| Scottish Government (Scotland council tax, guidance) | OGL v3.0 | Same as above | Same |
| nidirect, Department for Communities (NI) | Crown copyright; OGL v3.0 | Same as above | Same |
| Rent Smart Wales, councils | Facts only (scheme status, areas, dates); not copyright | Cite the source | Own wording, source URL and check date for each fact; no council text, logos or maps copied |

The National Archives states that the OGL v3.0 terms are compatible with CC BY 4.0: when OGL information is adapted and released under CC BY 4.0, the OGL conditions are met as long as the OGL attribution is kept (https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/). Reusers of the compiled data should keep the attribution statements below.

Not covered: logos, personal data and third-party material, which are excluded from the OGL. None are included.
